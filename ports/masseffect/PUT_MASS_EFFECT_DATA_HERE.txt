Put your own Mass Effect Infiltrator v1.0.58 (gamepad build) APK, ZIP, or
extracted folder in this directory. The filename does not matter.

Mass Effect Infiltrator PortMaster port and eapx extractor by EapRules.

The port validates this exact native library before extracting anything:

  lib/armeabi/libMassEffect.so
  SHA1 ea58b733d3d267ab639431b50539542faa43f0d0

The supported build is the gamepad edition, Android package
com.ea.games.meinfiltrator_gamepad. Backups are commonly labelled:

  Mass Effect Infiltrator Mobile Version 1.0.58
  Mass Effect Infiltrator vita 1.0.58

The known Vita-ready masseffect.zip is also supported. It contains the main
campaign but omits optional Survival, Burst Rifle and ~2x UI data. README.md
records its exact archive identity and the difference from a complete donor.

Do not place the PortMaster release zip here. That zip belongs in PortMaster's
autoinstall folder. See README.md for the complete installation instructions.
